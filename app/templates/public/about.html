About Norzagaray College Library

Empowering minds through knowledge, innovation, and accessible learning resources since 2007.

Our Vision

Norzagaray College envisions itself to transform lives of individuals and communities through life-long learning and productivity.

Our Mission

As an Institution of Higher Education, we commit ourselves to:

  • 1. Produce local and global competitive professionals through quality, accessible and affordable education;
  • 2. Develop the intellectual, physical, social, spiritual, cultural and career potential of the individual capable of meeting the needs of industry, public service and civil society;
  • 3. Provide an innovative educational environment, opportunities and experiences that enable individuals and communities to grow, thrive and prosper.

Our Journey

A legacy of excellence in education since 2004

December 21, 2004

Foundation Announced

Hon. Matilde A. Legaspi announced the establishment of a community college to provide quality, affordable education to marginalized sectors.

2006

Official Establishment

Through Sangguniang Bayan Ordinance No. 2006-10, Norzagaray College was officially established at the Municipal Compound.

March - June 2007

CHED Recognition & First Classes

CHED recognized NC as a Local Community College. Started with 680 students offering BS Computer Science, HRM, Nursing, BSEd, and BEEd programs.

2010-2011

Program Permits Granted

CHED granted permits for BSCS, BSHRM, BEEd, BSEd, and later the Nursing Program Certificate of Compliance.

January 29, 2019

Gawad Parangal Award

Received CHED's Gawad Parangal, making NC eligible for benefits under RA 10931 (Universal Access to Quality Tertiary Education Act).

February 21, 2025

Full Program Compliance

Obtained COPC for all program offerings: BSEd-Science, BSHM, BEEd, BSCS, and Associate in Computer Technology.
